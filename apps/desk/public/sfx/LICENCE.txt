What was changed
----------------
A subset was selected and the files were converted from Ogg Vorbis to AAC
(.m4a). Ogg is not safe to rely on in Safari, and a soundboard that is silent
on the teacher's iPad is not a soundboard. Nothing else was altered.

Why only one source
-------------------
Everything bundled here comes from Kenney because the provenance is
unambiguous: one author, one CC0 dedication, stated on the pack itself. Sites
that aggregate "free" sound effects mix licences per file — Pixabay, for
instance, moved off CC0 to its own licence in 2019 — and a classroom app is
the wrong place to be casual about that.

Anything not covered here is what the upload button is for: a teacher can add
their own sounds, and those stay private to them.
