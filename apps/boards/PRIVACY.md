## 1. Who this policy is for and our role

Signify Learning is a classroom whiteboard tool that teachers and students use
together. It is made available to **schools and educators** ("the School"). In
most cases the School decides who may use the Service and directs what student
information is entered into it.

- For student personal information, we generally act as a **service provider /
  data processor** on behalf of the School, which is the **data controller** and
  is responsible for obtaining any parental or guardian consent required by law.
- We process that information only to provide the Service, as described below and
  as instructed by the School.

If you are a parent or student with questions, please contact your School first;
they administer the account and control the data.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what the Service needs to work. We do **not** run third-party
advertising, we do **not** use advertising trackers, and we do **not** sell or
rent personal information.

**a. Account information (via Google Sign-In).** When you sign in with Google, we
receive and store your **email address** and **display name**, and we use Google's
verification that the email is valid. We request only the basic "sign in" scopes
(`openid`, `email`, `profile`). We do **not** access your Google Drive, contacts,
calendar, or other Google data.

**b. School / roster information.** An administrator may set or store: your
**role** (student, teacher, or administrator), your **grade level**, the
**classrooms** you belong to, and account **status** (active, pending, suspended,
blocked). Administrators may also add an account by email address before that
person first signs in.

**c. Content you create.** Whiteboard **boards** and everything you place on them:
drawings, text, shapes, charts, and **images or documents you upload** (PDF, Word,
PowerPoint, etc.). It also includes **comments** attached to board items and
**messages** you send to or receive from a teacher or classmate within a class.

**d. Usage and technical information.** To operate and secure the Service we
process: a **"last seen" timestamp**, connection and session data, and a
short-lived **session token** stored in your browser after you sign in (used to
keep you logged in; it expires after about 12 hours). Administrators can see an
**audit log** of administrative actions (for example, changing a role or grade,
or approving a template) and a basic **usage summary** (counts such as active
users and boards).

**e. Error reports.** If the app encounters an error in your browser, it may send
a short **automatic error report** to our server log containing the error message,
the page path, and the signed-in email address, so we can diagnose and fix
problems. These reports are not used for any other purpose.

We do not intentionally collect Social Security numbers, financial account
numbers, precise geolocation, or special-category/sensitive data, and you should
not enter such information into boards.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- authenticate you and keep you signed in;
- create, save, sync, and display boards, including **live collaboration** so
  edits appear on other participants' screens in real time;
- let teachers see and manage their students' boards, run polls, share or
  "spotlight" a board to a class, and push content to students;
- enable messaging and comments within a class;
- let administrators manage users, roles, grade levels, classrooms, templates,
  and school-wide settings;
- maintain **version history** and **backups** so work can be recovered;
- secure the Service, prevent abuse, and diagnose problems;
- comply with legal obligations.

## 5. How information is shared

We share information only as follows:

- **Within your School / class.** By design, teachers and administrators can view
  student boards and related content within their own school or classes. Board
  **content viewing is limited to your own school's email domain** — administrators
  cannot view boards belonging to a different school.
- **Boards you publish to the web.** A teacher or administrator may choose to
  **publish a specific board** to a public link (an embeddable, read-only view).
  When they do, **anyone with that link can view that board without signing in**,
  until it is unpublished. Do not publish boards containing information that
  should stay private. Only teachers and administrators can publish; students
  cannot, and this feature can be turned off school-wide, per class, or per
  student.
- **Service providers (sub-processors).** We use:
  - **Google** — for sign-in (Google Identity Services). See Google's Privacy
    Policy.
  - **Railway** — for cloud hosting and data storage. See Railway's Privacy
    Policy.
  Document conversion (e.g., Word/PowerPoint to PDF) is performed **on our own
  server** and files are not sent to a third-party conversion service.
- **Legal reasons.** We may disclose information if required by law, to protect
  rights and safety, or in connection with a merger or transfer of the Service
  (subject to this policy).

We do **not** sell personal information, and we do **not** share it for
advertising.

Signify Learning is intended for use **in schools, under the direction of

## 7. Data retention and deletion

- **Boards and account data** are retained while the account and class are active.
- **Version history** keeps a limited number of periodic snapshots of a board
  (approximately every 10 minutes of activity, a small fixed number of versions)
  so recent changes can be restored.
- **Backups** of the system are created on a rolling basis and retained for a
  limited period (approximately 7 days) before being overwritten.
- **Deletion.** When an administrator **deletes a user account**, we remove that
  user's account record, their boards (personal and class), and their class
  enrollments from active systems. Residual copies may persist in backups until
  those backups age out (up to about 7 days). Deleting a **classroom** removes its
  boards.
- A School may request deletion or export of its data by contacting
  [privacy@yourdomain].

## 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, including:

- **Encryption in transit** — all traffic to the Service uses HTTPS/TLS.
- **Access controls** — a role and permission system limits who can see and manage
  users and content; board content is scoped to a user's own school domain.
- **Authentication** — sign-in is handled by Google; our session tokens are
  cryptographically signed and expire.
- **Sandboxed public embeds** — a published board's public viewer can only view
  that one board and cannot reach any other data or account.
